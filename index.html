Summary

I am a Computer Engineering graduate from the University of Patras, with a strong focus on Machine Learning, Computer Vision, and Artificial Intelligence.

Additionally, I have experience with Python, NumPy, Pandas, Scikit-learn, and database management across SQL (MySQL, MariaDB) and NoSQL (MongoDB) systems. My portfolio includes data mining, predictive modeling, clustering, and full-stack web development using Node.js, Express, and JavaScript.
